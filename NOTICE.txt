It includes work from these projects.

html-explainer, by Derek Schwede (https://github.com/ds-vibe/html-explainer)
  The design system, the widget designs and their behaviour, the review overlay and chat dock
  (vendor/html-explainer/), and the seeded random number generator its pages use.

Tiny Little Office, by Paul Ford (https://github.com/aboard-io/tinylittleoffice)
  The idea of a document that lives in its link, the Markdown-with-fences source format, the
  rule that a link never runs code, and the link encoding conventions in lib/codec.js.
